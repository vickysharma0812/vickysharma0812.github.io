## Abstract

In this study, geothermal energy has shown immense potential to be considered as one of the sustainable and renewable energy resources for space conditioning. However, developing nation like India is yet to adopt this technology for space heating and cooling despite having satisfactory seasonal variation in the ambient temperature in many places. Additionally, the thermo-mechanical response of geothermal energy piles in local soil and climatic condition has remained unexplored. Therefore, an attempt has been made in the present study to numerically explore the thermo-mechanical response of geothermal energy pile in local soil under climatic condition of Jamshedpur, a city in Eastern India. From this study, the shear stress at the interface of the pile and adjacent soil, the displacement of pile head, middle and toe, and the volumetric strain of the soil have been analyzed. Therefore, the maximum displacement for mechanical loading was observed as 16 mm and 11.4 mm in sand and layered soil respectively, for L/d ratio 15. Under thermo-mechanical, change in displacement was 0.7 mm and 0.4 mm in sand and layered soil, respectively. Maximum shear stress was developed at toe, which was 60kN/m2 for L/d= 15. The obtained results reveal an encouraging outcome and helps in gaining confidence towards constructing geothermal energy piles.
