## Abstract

This paper proposes a modal analysis method based on the energy-based overset finite element method (EbO-FEM) for rigid frame bridges. Rigid frame bridges have complex geometries, which makes mesh generation for finite element analysis expensive. On the other hand, a nonconforming mesh is easily representable. The use of nonconforming meshes can significantly reduce the cost of mesh generation, therefore, we developed the EbO-FEM, one of the equation-based domain composition method and applied the scheme for the finite element modal analysis. First, a modal analysis of the cantilever beam was performed to verify the accuracy, and the EbO-FEM solution agreed well with all of the conventional FEM solutions, experimental results, and exact solutions. Second, the natural vibration modes and frequencies estimated for a real rigid frame bridge under construction by microtremor observation records and the Frequency Domain Decomposition (FDD) method, and the EbO-FEM reproduced its natural vibration modes and frequencies with high accuracy.
