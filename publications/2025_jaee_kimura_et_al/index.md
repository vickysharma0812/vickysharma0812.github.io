## Abstract

The preparation of a mesh is a costly procedure in finite element analyses. Energy-based overset finite element method (EbO-FEM) is a method that facilitates the creation of the mesh by composing several subdomains into a single computational domain. This paper presents a fundamental study to extend EbO-FEM to dynamic problems, focusing on the one-dimensional scalar wave problem. In particular, the relationship between the parameter representing the degree of coupling and the magnitude of the undesired reflected waves generated at the overlapping regions is investigated. The results show that EbO-FEM provides as accurate a solution as the conventional FEM when adopting well-tuned values as the parameter.
