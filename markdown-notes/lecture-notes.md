# Lecture Notes Overview

This document contains an overview of various lecture notes.

- Location of lecture notes: OneDrive/Knowledge/Lectures
- Shortcut is lectures
- The lectures are located in github.com/poromechanics

## Basics-FEM github.com/poromechanics/basics-fem

 - These are introduction to FEM to engineering students.
 - Here we explain the practical theory of finite element methods.
 - Mathematics of FEM is very minimal.
 - We talk about conventional FEM shape functions.
 - These notes are prepared by reading the Zienkicwicz book, Szabo and Babushka, Cook et al. (Basically practical simple applications).
 - These notes are like first course in FEM

## Basis-FEM github.com/poromechanics/basis-fem
 - These lectures deals with the advance concepts in finite element methods.
 - A deeper insights into the meaning of finite element, basis functions, and degree of freedom is provided.
 - In a way, these lectures deals with the theory of advance finite element approximation and their implementation
 - Here we cover H1, HDiv, HCurl finite element method. The concept of conformity is discussed thoroughly.
 - These notes are prepared from the books by Solin et al, Demkowicz et al.

## Math-FEM
 - These lectures are related to the functional analysis in FEM
 - We also cover calculus of variation
 - Minimization principals

## Spectral-FEM
 - These are related to application of higher-order finite element methods
 - These lecture notes cover the application of spectral FEM in solid and fluid mechanics

## DG-FEM
 - These notes are related to the DG-FEM
 - We will study the Nodal DGFEM, DG-FEM for parabolic, elliptic problems
 - Focus on construction of numerical flux
 - Hybrid DGFEM and Mixed DGFEM

## VMS-FEM
 - These notes are related to the Variational Multiscale Methods
